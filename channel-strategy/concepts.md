# Phase 4: Channel concepts

Note: the repo already has **Pip & Pebble** (kids-style animated comedy Shorts, `channel/CHANNEL.md`). The concepts below are a **separate brand**, because mixing audiences hurts recommendations.

## Concept A: **"The Last Log"** · real mysteries, fact vs legend ★ CHOSEN (score 82)
- **Identity:** Every famous mystery has a myth version and a record version. We tell the myth cinematically, then open the archive and show what is actually documented. Calm, precise narrator. No hype, no fake "evidence".
- **Names:** The Last Log · Archive of the Unexplained · Record & Rumor
- **Audience:** English-speaking adults 25–54 (US/UK/CA/AU) who watch documentaries in the background and binge.
- **Language:** English first (highest RPM). Add Spanish and Persian voice tracks later via multi-language audio, on the same channel.
- **Visual identity:** Stylized and **non-photoreal**: ink-and-watercolor "archive illustration". Sepia paper, one accent color (signal red) for the "record" moments. Document overlays (dates, map pins) built in HyperFrames. Non-photoreal visuals reduce deception risk and become a recognizable signature.
- **Format:** Cold open on the myth's most eerie detail → "but that detail never happened" → three-act investigation → verdict and the open question that remains.
- **Pillars:** Vanishings · Ships & lighthouses · Lost expeditions · Hoaxes that fooled everyone.
- **Long-form:** 12–18 min, 1 per week.
- **Shorts:** 3 per long video: myth vs record (30s), the single strangest documented detail (20s), and a "one question nobody answered" cliffhanger (25s).
- **Workflow:** see `PRODUCTION-SYSTEM.md`.
- **Advantage:** Competitors retell legends; we verify them. Citing sources builds trust, makes the content "authentic" under YouTube policy, and is hard to mass-produce.
- **Monetization:** AdSense (long-form), memberships (bonus case files), sponsorships (history apps, audiobooks).
- **Risks:** Research time (2–4 h per episode). Factual errors damage trust (mitigation: a source list in every description). Dark topics may get limited ads (mitigation: avoid gore and recent tragedies with living victims).

## Concept B: "Ink & Myth" · ancient epics in painterly animation (72)
Myths, folklore and sacred stories told as 15–25 min animated epics. A broad international audience. Risks: religious sensitivity, and many See-U-style competitors. The strong upside is shown by Bible Chronicles.

## Concept C: "Small Choices" · original karma / consequence Shorts (64)
Original 2D characters in 45–60s moral stories. The fastest reach (the onca pattern), but low RPM and heavy clone saturation. Best used later as a **Shorts feeder**, not as the main channel.

## Why A
It has the highest score, long-form revenue, natural Shorts spin-offs and real defensibility (research and sourcing). The visuals need only AI stills plus camera moves, so the cost per episode stays low. It also fits the HyperFrames + FFmpeg + Whisper pipeline already installed.
