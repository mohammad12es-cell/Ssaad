## Description (YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info)
Every famous mystery has two versions: the legend everyone repeats, and the record nobody reads.

The Last Log tells the legend, opens the archive, and follows the evidence. Vanished lighthouse keepers, ghost ships, lost expeditions and hoaxes that fooled the world, checked against the sources and with every source listed in the description.

New case file every week. Calm narration, illustrated reconstructions, no fake evidence.

Know a mystery where the famous clue might be fake? Tell us in the comments. It might become our next file.

*Illustrations are AI-assisted reconstructions, not historical images.*

## Settings checklist (YouTube Studio)
| Where | Setting |
|---|---|
| Customization → Basic info | Name, handle, description, keywords as above |
| Customization → Branding | Upload `avatar.png` and `banner.png`. Video watermark: `avatar.png` (from the end of the video) |
| Customization → Layout | Channel trailer: EP01 Short s2 · Featured video for returning viewers: the EP01 long-form |
| Settings → Upload defaults | Category: Education · Language: English · Licence: Standard · Tags: the last log, unsolved mysteries, history |
| Settings → Channel → Advanced | Audience: **No, not made for kids** (adult documentary) |
| Each upload → Altered content | **No** for stylized illustration. **Yes** if any shot could pass for real footage or a real photo |
| Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility | Verify your phone number (unlocks custom thumbnails and videos over 15 min) |
