## Content strategy (based on trending English animated Shorts, Nov 2026)
- **What is working:** original recurring animal duos doing quick slapstick, like the funny-frog cartoons that took channels with 500 subscribers to 1.6M views. Also cute "tough guy, soft heart" moments and "wait for the end" twists.
- **Avoid:** fan animations of game, film or TV characters (Deadlock, Pokémon, Simpsons and so on). They trend, but they carry copyright and claim risk.
- **Format:** 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds, a hook in the first second, a twist at the end, no dialogue (sound effects and music only) so the jokes work in every language.
- **Pipeline:** HyperFrames vector animation (no paid credits), an original synthesized soundtrack, titles like "Pip tried to fly 😂 #animation #cartoon #shorts".
- **Cadence:** 3 Shorts a week. Weekly analytics review: retention curve, swipe-away rate and traffic sources.
