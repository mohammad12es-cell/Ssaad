## Facts to double-check
1. Gaykhatu's chao notes were issued in Tabriz in 1294 and withdrawn after roughly two months (sources vary between weeks and months).
2. Penalties for refusing the notes are reported in contemporary sources (Rashid al-Din).
3. 'Almost 600 years' refers to the Imperial Bank of Persia's banknotes of around 1890.
