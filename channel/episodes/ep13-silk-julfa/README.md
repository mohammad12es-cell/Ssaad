## Facts to double-check
1. The deportation from Julfa began in 1604-1605 and caused great suffering; the number of people moved varies by source.
2. Shah Abbas's royal silk export monopoly dates to around 1619.
3. The network of New Julfa merchants reached Europe, India and Southeast Asia, including Manila.
