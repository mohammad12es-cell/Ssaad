## Facts to double-check
1. Bazaar (bāzār) and caravan (kārvān) come from Persian.
2. Check and checkmate come from chess terms rooted in Persian shāh; 'māt' is often explained as Persian/Arabic for helpless or defeated.
3. Cheque is usually traced to the Exchequer and its checkered counting cloth; details are debated by etymologists.
