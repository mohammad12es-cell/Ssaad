It comes from chess, and many chess words come from Persian.
Shah means king. When you threaten the king, you say: shah!
That became check. And shah mat, the king is helpless, became checkmate.
Medieval England counted taxes on a checkered cloth, like a chessboard.
That office was called the Exchequer.
And from there, we got the word cheque.
