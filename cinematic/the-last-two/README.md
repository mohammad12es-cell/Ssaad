# THE LAST TWO — 15-second dark-fantasy cinematic

Two samurai, one male and one female, stand alone on a rain-lashed battlefield against a horde of
orcs that stretches to the horizon. Everything in the film is procedural and original: the
geometry, materials, weather, lightning, camera and score are all generated by code in this folder.
It uses no stock footage, samples or AI video.

**Output:** `output/the-last-two-cinematic.mp4` is 15.000 s at 1920×1080 (16:9), 24 fps, H.264 (CRF 14),
with 48 kHz stereo AAC at 320k. It has no text, logos or subtitles.

## Shot breakdown

| Time | Shot | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Extreme wide from behind, slow push through heavy rain | Two tiny figures; the horde is a black wall of axes, pikes and crimson banners on the rise. Distant lightning at 1.5 s. |
| 4–8 s | The move continues into a slow orbit around the pair | A strike behind the army at 4.9 s lights the whole host; wet lacquer and blades flare. |
| 8–12 s | Low-angle hero close-up over their shoulders | He tightens his grip (8.9 s). She raises her katana from low guard to middle guard (9.7–11.3 s). The front ranks begin the charge in a wave that runs back through the rows (from 9.0 s). |
| 12–15 s | Massive flash (11.9 s) hides a cut behind the pair. The camera pulls back and rises. | The charge is revealed with the pair standing side by side. A final strike and bass impact land at 14.42 s. |

Shots 1–3 are one continuous Catmull-Rom camera move with no cuts. The only cut sits inside the
white-out of the great flash. Motion blur is real temporal sampling with a 180° shutter. Each
subframe is also sub-pixel jittered, which doubles as anti-aliasing.

## Sound (synthesized in `audio.py`)

The score is built from these layers:
- Rain and wind beds.
- Thunder (crack plus rolling rumble) timed to every lightning event, with a distance delay.
- A low D-minor string drone with a cello motif.
- A brass swell from 7.6 s.
- A climax chord with tremolo strings and a choir-like pad after the flash.
- Taiko war drums accelerating from 8 s to the end.
- Distant orc war cries and the stampede rumble from 9.3 s.
- Foley: lamellar armour clinks, a leather grip creak and the katana "shing".
- A sub-bass impact on the final strike.

The master is about −14.6 LUFS with a −0.6 dBTP peak.

## Rebuild

```bash
./build.sh            # ~45 min on 4 CPU cores (software WebGL); writes output/the-last-two-cinematic.mp4
node render.mjs --preview 0,5,10,12.5 --w 960 --h 540   # quick stills in ./preview
```

`render.mjs` serves this folder, drives `window.renderFrame(t)` in headless Chromium at exact
1/24 s steps and pipes the frames to ffmpeg. `--mb N` sets the number of motion-blur subframes.

## Files

- `scene.js` holds the whole world:
  - Samurai rigs: lamellar armour, 2-bone IK arms on the katana grip, and wind-driven hair and hachimaki.
  - The instanced orc horde: thousands of warriors in 3 weapon variants and 2 LODs, plus banners.
  - Terrain with wet-mud puddles.
  - Three rain layers, splashes and ground mist.
  - Procedural lightning bolts and a storm-cloud sky.
  - The camera path and the grade (cold steel with kept crimson, vignette, grain).
- `audio.py` builds the score and sound design.
- `render.mjs` is the deterministic frame renderer.
- `lib/` holds three.js r170 and the post-processing passes.
