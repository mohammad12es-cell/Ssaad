## Repeated success patterns (seen in 3+ channels)
1. **Promise a resolved question.** Mystery, "why did", "what happened to". (Trust Me Bro, History Matters, Did You Know ?, Discovered Files, Top5s.)
2. **Emotion over information.** Karma, injustice, rescue. (onca, Akixin, Animated Stories.)
3. **Familiar subject, fresh angle.** Bible, myths, famous mysteries: proven search demand with a new frame. (Bible Chronicles, See U, Trust Me Bro.)
4. **A distinct narrator voice or style beats volume.** History Matters and Trust Me Bro outperform the high-volume channels per upload.
5. **Long-form = revenue, Shorts = reach.** None of the top earners is Shorts-only.

## Do not copy
Distinctive characters, narrator persona (Trust Me Bro's humor), "files" branding, re-animated third-party audio (Spacestation), and fan IP.
