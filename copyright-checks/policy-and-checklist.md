# Phase 5: Copyright and monetization safety

**No workflow guarantees zero claims or YPP approval.** This reduces the risk; it does not remove it.

- YouTube channel monetization policies (incl. *inauthentic content*, renamed from "repetitious" on 2025-07-15): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- YPP overview and eligibility: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851

(I confirmed the disclosure page and the July 2025 update through search results. The other URLs are YouTube's standard help pages and should be checked before relying on them.)

## What the 2025 update actually means (per coverage)
AI is allowed. **Mass-produced or templated content with little variation is not monetizable, and the penalty applies to the whole channel.** Reused-content rules were unchanged. Realistic synthetic content must be disclosed at upload.

## Per-video checklist (copy into each episode folder)
- [ ] The script was written from primary or secondary sources, not from another video's script. Sources are listed in the description.
- [ ] No downloaded third-party video or audio. Archival images must be public domain (pre-1930 or a government source) and logged with a URL.
- [ ] All visuals were generated by us. The tool's plan allows commercial use (log the tool + plan + date).
- [ ] No real living person depicted in a realistic way. No celebrity voices.
- [ ] Voice: our own recording, or a TTS voice whose licence allows commercial use (log it).
- [ ] Music and SFX from a licensed library (YouTube Audio Library, or a paid licence with a stored certificate) or our own synthesis.
- [ ] "Altered content = Yes" if any realistic-looking scene could be mistaken for real footage.
- [ ] The episode differs meaningfully from the previous ones (new structure beats, new visual set pieces).
- [ ] No fabricated "evidence" (fake documents, fake photos presented as real). Reconstructions are labeled on screen as "Illustration".
- [ ] Thumbnail is original and contains no other creator's branding.
