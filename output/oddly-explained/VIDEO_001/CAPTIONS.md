Your brain never simply switches off. 🧠✨

Every night it moves through coordinated stages:
🌊 NREM sleep: brain-wave patterns shift from stage to stage
💭 REM sleep: activity becomes more wake-like, and vivid dreams are especially common (dreams can happen in NREM too)
📚 Meanwhile, sleep helps your brain process memories and support learning.

And that's only part of what happens while you sleep.
