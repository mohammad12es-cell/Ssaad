**Caption:**
In your dream, you're running. Your body isn't. 🧠⚡

During REM sleep, circuits in your brainstem shut down signals to most of your muscles. It's called REM atonia, a temporary paralysis that helps stop you from acting out your dreams.

👀 Your eyes keep darting.
🫁 Your diaphragm keeps you breathing.

When this system fails, some people physically act out their dreams. It's a sleep disorder called REM sleep behavior disorder.
