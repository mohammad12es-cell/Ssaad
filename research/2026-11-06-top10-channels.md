# Phase 1: Top 10 channels with AI-reproducible formats

**Research date:** 2026-11-06
**Data source:** vidIQ channel index (2 searches, data current to 2026-11-03). Counts for subscribers, views, videos, growth and uploads are **facts from the index**. "Est. earnings" is **vidIQ's estimate**, not real revenue.
**Limits of this research (read first):**
- youtube.com is blocked by this session's network policy, so I could not open channel pages, watch videos or read thumbnails. Everything about hooks, pacing and visual style in Phase 2 is a **hypothesis** until someone watches the videos.
- vidIQ credits ran out after 2 of 4 planned searches. The "What If / science" and "stickman" niches were **not searched**. Re-run those when credits are back.
- **AI use is not verifiable for any channel in the top 10.** No channel publicly says which tools it uses. They are selected because their format *can* be reproduced with AI tools, not because they are proven AI channels. Two channels say the opposite: People Profiles says its scripts are human-written (2026), and onca says it uses "writers, voice actors, and animators".

## Selection logic
I sorted about 30 channels by **recent momentum** (30-day views, 1-year growth), not by subscribers. I dropped channels with flat or negative growth (Mind Boggler, Eskify, YASS, Avocado Couple) and channels built on someone else's IP (Sami Trigger is Minecraft/Roblox-based).

**Takeaway (hypothesis):** in this sample, the channels that *market themselves* as AI do worst. The winners sell the **story**, and the tool doesn't matter to viewers.

## Key facts for strategy
1. **Long-form mystery and documentary earns the most per view.** Did You Know ? makes an estimated $60.7K/mo on 10M monthly views. Akixin makes an estimated $0.3K/mo on 4.4M Shorts views. Shorts ad revenue (RPM) is much lower.
2. **Momentum leaders are new channels** (2023–2025): onca, Trust Me Bro, Discovered Files, Bible Chronicles. The market is not closed.
3. **Volume ≠ efficiency.** History Matters got 5.9M views in 30 days from 1 video. See U in History got 2.0M from 42. Quality per upload wins.
