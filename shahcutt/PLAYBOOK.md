## 2. The formula (update it as new data arrives)

1. **Persian**, spoken + on-screen. English reels lost 65–70% of viewers at the start.
2. **Frame 0 = one big sentence that opens a question** (confession or secret): «منم اول باورم نمی‌شد…», «هیچکس این رو بهت نمی‌گه…». Never a logo, price, UI card or greeting first.
3. **Emotion before offer**: curiosity, surprise or a personal confession; the service/price never leads.
4. **One idea, 3 steps or 1 story**, 30–50 s. The answer is completed only near the end (open loop).
5. **Real numbers from his own work** (skip rates, follower growth 0→12K / 0→22K, 12 videos a week) make it believable.
6. **Comment-keyword CTA** in the last 5 s, one word, tied to a free, specific gift (page review, hook rewrite, checklist).
7. Visual: dark cinematic ground, one hot accent (red / amber glow), huge Persian type, motion that proves he is a motion designer.

## 4. CTA, caption and DM rules

- Keyword: one short Persian word, new each reel (هوک، ادیت، پیج، تحلیل…).
- Caption: line 1 repeats the hook; 2–4 short lines of value; CTA line with the keyword; 3–6 Persian hashtags (#تدوین #موشن_گرافیک #ریلز #تولید_محتوا #اینستاگرام_مارکتینگ).
- DM: thank them, deliver the promised gift or ask the 2 questions needed to deliver it, invite them to send the page link, one soft line about paid editing, sign as Shahin.
- DMs are sent by Shahin or by an Instagram-approved tool (for example ManyChat keyword automation); Claude only writes them.

## 5. Daily loop

1. Read the last reels' insights (free): plays, avg watched %, skip rate, shares, saves. Note what moved.
2. Research outliers when vidIQ credits exist (renew on the 2nd of each month, 150 credits; 1 outlier search = 5): Persian/English editing, motion-design and content-growth reels.
3. Pick today's idea + hook from the formula; write the script (30–50 s, Persian), beats, CTA keyword.
4. Build with `shahcutt/engine` (new_reel.py), Gemini voice, render, cover.
5. Send the draft (video, cover, caption, keyword, DM text). **Publish only after Shahin approves.**

## 6. Strong-reel checklist (score every script before building; build only at 8/10 or more)

| # | Part | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook, 0–1.5 s | Frame 0 shows a big sentence that opens a gap (confession, secret, mistake, bold number); the first spoken words say the same thing; no greeting, logo or price |
| 2 | Hook proof, 1.5–4 s | A concrete number, result or visual proves the hook is worth staying for |
| 3 | Body promise | The viewer knows by 5 s what they will get by the end (3 steps, 1 story, a before→after) |
| 4 | Re-hooks | Every 5–7 s a new beat: number, contrast, "but…", a step counter, a visual change |
| 5 | Open loop | The best part (the result, step 3, the reveal) is held for the last third |
| 6 | Value | A viewer can apply one thing today without buying anything |
| 7 | Proof | Real numbers or real work from Shahin; nothing invented |
| 8 | CTA | One keyword, tied to a specific free gift, said and shown in the last 5 s |
| 9 | Length & pace | 30–45 s, no pause over 0.35 s, a visual change at least every 3 s |
| 10 | Loop / share | The last line or frame makes rewatching or sending to a friend natural |

## 7. Where the formulas come from (no bot browsing of Instagram)
