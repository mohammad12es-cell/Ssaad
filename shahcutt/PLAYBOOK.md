## 3. Constant rules (still true)

1. **Persian**, spoken + on-screen.
2. **Frame 0 carries the hook** (the result, the mistake or a big sentence). Never a logo, price or greeting first.
3. **One idea**, 25–45 s; the payoff completed only near the end.
4. **Proof is real**: his own work on screen, or numbers from his real results (clinics 0→~12K and 0→~22K followers, 12 videos a week, every two-week review above 8/10). Nothing invented.
5. **CTA**: one comment keyword (new each reel) tied to a concrete gift (P7).
6. Visual: dark cinematic ground, one hot accent (red / amber glow), huge Persian type, motion that proves he is a motion designer.
7. Rotate patterns: not the same main pattern two days in a row.

## 5. CTA, caption and DM rules

- Keyword: one short Persian word, new each reel. Used: دیزنی، رونالدو، پیج، هوک، ادیت.
- Caption: line 1 repeats the hook; 2–4 short lines of value; CTA line with the keyword; 3–6 Persian hashtags (#تدوین #موشن_گرافیک #ریلز #تولید_محتوا #اینستاگرام_مارکتینگ).
- DM: thank them, deliver the promised gift in the DM itself, one soft line about paid editing, sign «شاهین | @shahcutt».
- DMs are sent by Shahin or by an Instagram-approved tool (for example ManyChat keyword automation); Claude only writes them.

## 6. Daily loop

1. Read the sources in §1 (reports first). Log every strong example in §8; update §2 (patterns in 2+ niches).
2. Pick today's pattern (not yesterday's) and map it to editing / motion design.
3. Write the script (Persian, 25–45 s), score it on §7, rewrite until 8+.
4. Build with `shahcutt/engine` (new_reel.py), Gemini voice, render, cover.
5. Send the draft (video, cover, caption, keyword, DM, the source reels). **Publish only after Shahin approves.**
6. One line on the page's own numbers (sanity check only).

## 7. Strong-reel checklist (score every script before building; build only at 8/10 or more)

| # | Part | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook, 0–1.5 s | Frame 0 shows the hook (result, mistake, big sentence); the first spoken words say the same thing; no greeting, logo or price |
| 2 | Hook proof, 1.5–4 s | A concrete result, contrast or visual proves the hook is worth staying for |
| 3 | Body promise | The viewer knows by 5 s what they will get by the end (3 steps, 1 story, a before→after) |
| 4 | Re-hooks | Every 3–6 s a new beat: number, contrast, "but…", a step counter, a visual change |
| 5 | Open loop | The best part (the result, step 3, the reveal) is held for the last third |
| 6 | Value | A viewer can apply one thing today without buying anything |
| 7 | Proof | Real work or real numbers from Shahin; nothing invented |
| 8 | CTA | One keyword, tied to a concrete gift, said and shown in the last 5 s |
| 9 | Length & pace | 25–45 s, no pause over 0.35 s, a visual change at least every 3 s |
| 10 | Loop / share | The last line or frame makes rewatching or sending to a friend natural |
| + | Trend fit | Built on a pattern from §2 that is live this week (write which one) |
