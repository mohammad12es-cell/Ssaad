## Research summary
Six techniques behind the recent high-performing reels in the editing / motion-design / AI-creator niche:
1. **The AI-made reveal**: "AI made this" plus the result in the first 2 s.
2. **Comment keyword + free file**: "comment X and I'll DM you the prompt/checklist".
3. **The secret tool**: a named tool the viewer doesn't know yet.
4. **Savable series**: "part 1 of 3", which drives saves and follows.
5. **Sound design**: silence and hits used as punctuation.
6. **AI vs hand-made debate**: invites comments either way.

Common traits: a 2 s result-first hook, 15–35 s length, a loop ending, the CTA both on screen and in the caption.
