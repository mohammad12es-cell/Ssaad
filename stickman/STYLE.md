# Stickman series: "Inky" style guide

## Look
- **Background:** notebook paper `#fbf7ea`, blue rules every 64 px, red margin line at x=120.
- **Ink:** `#1d1d2b`, 9 px round strokes. Accent red `#e63946`, highlight yellow `#ffe45c`.
- **Line boil:** SVG turbulence displacement (scale 5) with the seed tweened 0→12/s, so lines wobble like hand drawing.
- **Hero:** Inky, a classic stick figure (head r=55, dot eyes, a mouth in 3 states: smile, worry, "O").
- **Font:** Patrick Hand (OFL). Captions are yellow sticker boxes with a 7 px ink border, tilted −2°, at y≈200.
- **Signature move:** things get *drawn* on screen (stroke dash and a pencil tracing the line) and *erased* (mask).

## Format
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 15–20 s. Hook on frame 0, a new beat every 2–3 s, a twist in the last 5 s.
- No dialogue: sound effects plus an original synthesized tune (`make_audio.py`).

## Copyright rules
- Original characters only: no Alan Becker, Stick War, Henry Stickmin or game characters.
- No commercial music or samples. All audio is generated by `make_audio.py`.
- Fonts are OFL (Patrick Hand), GSAP is free to use.

## Episodes
1. `ep01-draw-battle`: Drawing Battle (the eraser takes his legs, so he draws giant ones).
2. `ep02-ink-blot`: Ink Blot (erasing makes it split, the hole spits out a giant blot, he escapes through a margin door).
3. `ep03-draw-a-friend`: Draw a Friend (his friend draws "Inky 2.0" and leaves with him, Plan B is a dog that eats the pencil).

## Building an episode
- Shared rig and helpers: `shared/inky.js` (copy it into the episode's `assets/`). Shared synth: `shared/sfx.py`.
- Copy an existing episode folder, change `index.html` and `make_audio.py`, then run `python3 make_audio.py` and `npx hyperframes render`.
- Scale SVG groups with an attribute tween (`attr: { transform: "scale(...)" }`) when the pivot must be an exact point. A GSAP `transformOrigin` on a group scaled from 0 drifts.
