# Phase 3: Opportunity scoring

**Data caveat:** vidIQ keyword and trend data were unavailable (out of credits), and YouTube is blocked. The scores combine the **channel-growth facts** from Phase 1 with **judgment (hypothesis)**. Re-score with `vidiq_keyword_research mode=rising` once credits are back.

## Scoring model (0–100)
| Factor | Weight | 10 = |
|---|---|---|
| Demand (search + browse appetite) | 20 | Proven by multiple growing channels |
| Low competition / saturation | 15 | Few quality channels |
| Production ease with AI | 15 | Stills + narration are enough |
| Retention potential | 15 | Natural open loops |
| Recurring-episode potential | 10 | Endless topic supply |
| Monetization (RPM, advertiser-safe) | 15 | Long-form, adult, advertiser-friendly |
| Shorts + long-form fit | 10 | Each long video yields 3+ Shorts |

Score = Σ(factor score/10 × weight).
